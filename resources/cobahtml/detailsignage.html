01 — Overview

What is Digital Signage?

Digital Signage is a system for distributing visual content through a network of smart electronic displays — LED, LCD, video wall, or interactive kiosks — centrally managed from a single dashboard. ZD One Platform enables marketing, operations, and IT to schedule, push, and monitor content across hundreds of locations without the need for physical visits.

More than just a "big TV", modern digital signage is a strategic communication medium: displaying promotions based on time, conveying safety information in factories, running queue displays in hospitals, or serving as interactive wayfinding in malls. All content can be personalized by location, by hour, and even by audience through integration with data analytics.

02 — Problems We Solve

Communication problems
across multiple locations.

Without a centralized signage system, marketing campaigns are delayed, important information doesn't reach the frontline, and content production budgets are inefficient.

Problem 01

Content updates are slow & manual

The marketing team has to send promo files via USB/email to each branch. During a 6-hour flash sale campaign, many branches haven't had time to update content — sales opportunities are lost.

Problem 02

Content is not relevant per location

Promo Bali is displayed in the Medan branch. Product prices that differ per city appear incorrectly. Personalization per region requires granular control not available in legacy systems.

Problem 03

Unknown screen hangs

Digital displays at branches go offline or freeze, but it's only noticed when staff pass by. Meanwhile, customers see blank screens for hours — damaging the brand image.

Problem 04

No ROI tracking

There is no concrete data on whether signage content is actually displayed, at what time, and how often. Marketing struggles to evaluate campaigns without proof-of-play reports.
