01 — Overview

What is Endpoint Management?

Endpoint management is a system to centralize control, security, and lifecycle of every laptop and workstation across your organization — from procurement to retirement. ZD One Platform allows IT teams to enroll new devices, deploy applications, enforce security policies, and remediate issues — all without ever physically touching the hardware.

More than just an inventory tool, modern endpoint management is a strategic IT control plane: pushing security patches the same hour they're released, deploying enterprise software to thousands of laptops in one tap, and isolating compromised devices from the network in seconds. Every laptop becomes a managed, compliant, observable asset.

Zero-touch deploy

Pre-configure new laptops before users open the box.

Policy enforcement

Security baselines applied automatically per role.

Continuous patching

Auto-update OS & apps with staged rollout control.

02 — Problems We Solve

IT chaos
in a hybrid workforce.

Without centralized endpoint management, IT teams burn hours on manual setup, security gaps go unnoticed, and software licenses leak into untracked spend.

Problem 01

New laptop setup takes 4–6 hours each

IT staff manually install OS, drivers, antivirus, productivity suite, and corporate apps for every new hire. With 50 onboardings per month, that's 250+ hours wasted on repetitive setup work.

Problem 02

Security patches lag for weeks

Critical CVEs are patched at HQ but lag in branch offices and remote workers. Vulnerable laptops keep operating with known security flaws — making the whole network exposed to ransomware.

Problem 03

Lost or stolen laptops carry sensitive data

A field engineer's laptop gets stolen at the airport. It has client documents, VPN credentials, and customer data. Without remote wipe capability, the data breach risk turns into a regulatory nightmare.

Problem 04

Software license waste & shadow IT

Adobe licenses sit unused on offboarded employees' laptops. Teams install unauthorized SaaS without IT approval. There's no single source of truth for what software is running where — and how much it's costing.
